# Scope rules

/client-onboarding and /scope-check compare client requests against these rules
and the client's contract. Edit them to match how {{AGENCY_NAME}} sells.

## The contract wins

If the call, the proposal or an email promises something the contract does not
include, it is out of scope until the contract changes. Flag it as an open
question for the account lead. Never quietly absorb it.

## Things that are always a change request

- A new brand, product line, region or business unit
- A new channel or platform
- Work the contract lists under exclusions
- More deliverables per month than the contract states
- A deadline earlier than the contract timeline

## Things that are in scope by default

- Reporting on the work we are doing
- Fixing problems in work we delivered
- Advice in the course of normal account management

## How to raise it

Write the request, the contract clause it conflicts with, and two options:
decline it, or quote it as a change request. The account lead decides.
