# Onboarding checklist

/client-onboarding checks every new client against this list. Edit it to match
how {{AGENCY_NAME}} actually onboards. Each line is one check. Mark a check
`[blocking]` when delivery cannot start until it is resolved.

## Documents we need before kickoff

- [blocking] Signed contract or statement of work
- Proposal that was accepted
- Sales or discovery call transcript or notes
- Brand guidelines (logo, colours, voice)

## Access we need

- [blocking] Admin or analyst access to every account named in scope
- Analytics or reporting access
- CRM read access, so results can be tied to revenue

## People we need named

- [blocking] One approver who can sign off deliverables
- Day-to-day contact
- Billing contact

## Things to confirm in writing

- [blocking] Every brand, product or business unit we will work on is named in the contract
- The primary goal and the metric it is judged on
- Anything the client expects that the contract excludes
- Launch dates or events we are working toward
- [blocking] No contract deadline (access, start date, deliverables) has already passed unmet
- Any live spend or work on products the contract excludes, and what happens to it
