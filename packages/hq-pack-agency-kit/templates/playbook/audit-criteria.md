# Audit criteria

Step 3 of /client-onboarding audits the client's current setup against these
criteria. Rewrite them for the services {{AGENCY_NAME}} sells. Every finding
must cite the file and row or section it came from.

## Goal fit

- What does the client say they want (from the call and the contract)?
- What is the current setup actually optimizing for?
- If those differ, that is the headline finding.

## Measurement

- Is the main conversion tied to revenue, or only to an early signal such as a form fill?
- Are there gaps in the data (missing months, missing accounts, untracked channels)?
- Do the exports cover the same period? Never divide numbers from one period by
  numbers from another (for example, quarterly spend by one month of sales).
- Can results be followed from first touch to closed deal? If not, say where the trail ends.

## Performance

- Spend, results and cost per result for each campaign or channel, calculated from the export.
- Which segments produce the customers the client actually wants?
- Which spend produces results that never turn into revenue?

## Assets

- Do the landing pages, offers and calls to action match the customer the client wants?
- What is missing that we would need to fix the gaps above?

## Honesty rule

When the data cannot support a conclusion, write "Not supported by the data
provided" and name the data that would settle it. Do not estimate to fill the gap.
