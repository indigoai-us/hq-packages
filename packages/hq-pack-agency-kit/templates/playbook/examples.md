## Example client brief

> **Client:** Example Co. (one brand: Example Pro)
> **Goal:** 40 qualified demos a month from companies with 200+ staff, judged on
> demos held, not forms filled. Source: contract section 2, call 00:14:10.
> **In scope:** paid search and LinkedIn for Example Pro. Source: contract section 3.
> **Out of scope:** website changes. Source: contract section 6.
> **Open questions:**
> 1. The call mentioned Example Lite. The contract names only Example Pro. Is Lite
>    in scope? Owner: account lead. Blocking.
> 2. No approver named. Owner: client. Blocking.

## Example creative brief

> **Deliverable:** 3 LinkedIn single-image ads for Example Pro
> **Audience:** IT directors at companies with 200+ staff. Source: contract section 2.
> **Message:** Example Pro replaces the weekly reporting scramble with one
> dashboard IT already trusts. Proof: the 40% time saving from the client's
> case study (inputs/case-study.pdf, page 2).
> **Call to action:** Book a 20-minute demo (not "start free trial").
> **Mandatories:** logo bottom left, brand blue, no customer names without approval.
> **Approver:** client marketing lead, after strategist review.

## Example delivery plan item

> **Test 1: move bidding from form fills to qualified demos**
> Why: 82% of last quarter's leads came from companies under 50 staff and none
> closed. Source: crm-outcomes.csv rows 2 to 41.
> What: import demo-held events from the CRM and bid on those.
> Depends on: CRM access (missing, requested in intake).
> Success measure: cost per qualified demo, reviewed after 30 days.
> Needs approval: strategist, then client.
