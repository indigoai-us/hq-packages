# Proposal: Northwind Labs paid acquisition

Practice client. Accepted 2026-09-18.

## What we heard

Northwind wants more enterprise customers. Paid campaigns bring in plenty of
leads, but sales says few of them are a fit.

## What we will do

1. Restructure Google and LinkedIn campaigns around enterprise buyers.
2. Rebuild conversion tracking so we optimize toward sales-qualified pipeline.
3. Refresh ad creative for the enterprise message.

## Optional phase 2

A landing page refresh with a "Book a demo" path for enterprise visitors. We
think this is needed to hit the goal and suggest starting it in month two.

## Investment

$8,000 per month, 90 days.
