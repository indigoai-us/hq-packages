A record month for orders, paid for in shipping speed

About

Fable & Loom is a fictional direct-to-consumer home-textiles brand. This monthly summarises operations for the leadership meeting on the first Monday of the month.

Method

Orders and revenue come from the Shopify order ledger at close of 31 August. On-time shipping counts orders scanned by the carrier within two business days. Return rate is returns received in August over orders shipped in July. Targets are the FY2026 operating plan.

Definitions

Watch — a leading indicator missed its weekly threshold. Off track — a plan metric missed for the week and no recovery is scheduled.
