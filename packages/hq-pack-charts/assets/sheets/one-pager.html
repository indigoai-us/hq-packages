About

Northwind Outfitters is a fictional outdoor-gear retailer with four stores and an online shop. This one-pager summarises its June 2026 customer survey for the leadership team.

Method

Email invitation to 3,150 customers who bought in the previous twelve months; 412 completed (13%). Satisfaction is the mean of a 0–100 slider per category. The 2024 comparison uses the same questions and a 388-response sample. Reason-to-buy allowed up to three picks.

Source

Survey export and cleaning notes: northwind-survey-2026.csv. Rows with fewer than three answered questions were excluded (17 rows).
